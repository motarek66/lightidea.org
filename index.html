About LightIdea

LightIdea is an esteemed Oracle Partner dedicated to empowering organizations with sustainable improvement and effective business performance management. We specialize in Oracle Fusion solutions spanning ERP, EPM, Risk Management, and Fusion Analytics Warehouse, combining proven methodologies with deep functional and technical expertise.

Our team is drawn from Big-4 and leading consulting firms, bringing together senior professionals with extensive experience in enterprise transformation, finance, supply chain, risk, and analytics. We serve organizations across the public and private sectors, delivering solutions that are practical, scalable, and aligned with real business objectives.

At LightIdea, we believe that technology alone does not drive transformation — it is the combination of the right solution, the right expertise, and the right support that creates lasting value. That belief shapes everything we do, from initial advisory engagements through full-scale implementation and long-term managed services.
